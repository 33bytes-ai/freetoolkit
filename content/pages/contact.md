## Get in touch

FounderCalc is a small, independently run site maintained by one person. If
you've found a bug, spotted a figure that's out of date, have a suggestion for
a new calculator, or have a question about how something works, I'd like to
hear from you.

**Email:** [{{ contact_email }}](mailto:{{ contact_email }})

Every message gets read. As a one-person project I can't always reply
individually, but reports that identify a concrete problem are acted on — and
corrections take priority over everything else on the list.

## Reporting a wrong result

This is the most useful kind of message to send, and the easiest to act on
when it includes the specifics. Please include:

- **The tool**, ideally as a link to its page.
- **The exact numbers you entered.** Most calculators have a "Share results"
  button that copies a URL with your inputs already filled in — pasting that
  link is the fastest way to show me precisely what you saw.
- **The result you got**, and the result you expected.
- **Why you expected it**, if there's a source — a provider's pricing page, an
  invoice, a textbook formula. This matters when a metric has more than one
  accepted definition, which is often the reason two figures disagree.

## Reporting an out-of-date fee or rate

Processor fees and tax rates change, and this site quotes a lot of them. If a
published rate here no longer matches the provider's own pricing page, send
the link to that page and it will be corrected.

## Reporting a bug

For something that behaves wrongly rather than calculates wrongly — a control
that won't accept input, a layout that breaks, a result that won't copy — it
helps to include:

- The tool you were using and a link to its page
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- Your browser and version, and your operating system
- Whether it happens consistently or only sometimes

## Suggesting a calculator

Most of the tools on this site started as someone's specific, recurring
annoyance. If you're repeatedly doing the same arithmetic in a spreadsheet,
describe the calculation and what decision you use it for. Suggestions that
explain the underlying problem are far more likely to turn into a good tool
than a bare request for a name.
