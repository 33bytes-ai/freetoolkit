## Put a calculator on your own site

Every calculator on FounderCalc can be embedded on your blog, your pricing page,
your help centre or a client proposal. Open any tool, click **Embed this
calculator on your site**, and paste the two lines of code where you want it to
appear. The calculator runs entirely in your visitor's browser: nothing they
type is sent to FounderCalc or anyone else, and it works on WordPress, Webflow,
Ghost and plain HTML.

The free embed is free for good. It carries a small credit line that links back
to the calculator's page here, and that is the whole cost.
