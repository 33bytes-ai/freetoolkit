## Summary

FounderCalc's calculators (Stripe fees, MRR, LTV/CAC, runway, profit margin,
freelance rates, and more) run **entirely in your browser**. Numbers or data
you enter into a calculator are processed locally using JavaScript and are
**never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers**.

This page explains what limited information our web server and any
advertising partners may collect when you visit the site.

## Server logs

Like virtually all websites, our web server automatically records standard
technical information for each request, including:

- The page requested and the time of the request
- Your browser's user agent string
- A truncated/anonymized form of your IP address
- The referring page, if any

These logs are used solely to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. which tools
are popular, whether the site is working correctly) and to detect abuse. They
are not linked to any account, because no accounts exist, and are
periodically deleted.

## Cookies and advertising

This site carries display advertising served by **Google AdSense**. Google and
its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and
measure ads, and — where you have consented to it — to personalise those ads
based on your visits to this and other sites.

- Google's use of advertising cookies is described in
  [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can review and change your personalisation settings at any time at
  [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and opt out of
  personalised advertising from participating vendors at
  [YourAdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/) or
  [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) in the EU.

### Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message,
provided through **Google Funding Choices** (an IAB TCF-registered consent
platform), before any non-essential cookie is set. You can accept or refuse
personalised advertising there, and you can reopen that choice at any time
using the privacy or consent link in the page footer. Refusing does not
restrict access to any calculator on this site — the tools work identically
either way, since none of them depend on advertising or cookies to compute a
result.

No advertising cookie is set before that choice is made.

## Other third-party services

- **Cloudflare** hosts and delivers the site. It processes connection data
  (including your IP address) to serve pages and to protect the site from
  abuse — see [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Formspree** handles submissions if you choose to enter your email address
  in the optional newsletter form. Your address is sent to Formspree only when
  you submit that form, and is used only to send the newsletter you asked for
  — see [Formspree's privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

Nothing you type into a calculator is ever sent to any of these services.

## Data you enter into tools

Every calculator on FounderCalc processes your input **locally in your
browser**. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to FounderCalc's
servers, stored, or shared with any third party.

## Third-party links

Some pages may link to external resources for further reading. FounderCalc is
not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

## Your rights

The site is published from France, so the GDPR applies to personal data
processed here. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or
erasure of personal data relating to you, to object to or restrict its
processing, and to withdraw consent to advertising cookies at any time (see
above). Because this site holds no accounts and stores nothing you enter into
a calculator, in practice the only personal data connected to you is what
appears in server logs and, if you submitted it, a newsletter email address.

To make a request, or to complain about how data is handled, use the
[contact page](/contact/). Publisher details are on the
[legal notice](/legal-notice/) page. If you are in the EU you also have the
right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in
France, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/).

## Children

This site is aimed at people running businesses and is not directed at
children under 16. No personal data is knowingly collected from them.
